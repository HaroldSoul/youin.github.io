LET’S GET YOU BACK IN.

Need a hand?

A few things to try before your next round. These guides cover planned app features; exact menus may vary at launch.

Can I download the app yet?

We are preparing YouIN for iOS and Android. Release information and download links will appear on this website when they are ready.

The camera will not turn on.
  1. Check and allow YouIN’s camera permission in device settings. On iOS, look in the app list or Privacy & Security. On Android, look in the app’s permissions.
  2. Close other apps using the camera, then reopen YouIN.
  3. Make sure the lens is not covered and try again in a well-lit place where your face is visible.

Setting names vary by operating system version. Camera-based game features may be limited if you do not allow access.

I cannot select or load a photo.

Check YouIN’s photo access in device settings. If you allowed only selected photos, add the photo you want to use or select it again through the system photo picker. Choose only the photos you need when full-library access is unnecessary.

If a photo is stored only in the cloud, download it to your device and try again. Use another person’s photo only with their consent.

Last Kick cannot connect to a nearby friend.
  1. Bring both devices close together and open the same game’s nearby-connection screen.
  2. Check that Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are enabled.
  3. Review nearby-device, local-network, or other permissions requested by your operating system. Some Android versions may require location permission for nearby discovery.
  4. Confirm you are connecting to the right person. End the connection and try again.

If it still fails, restart both apps and check that the versions match. Nearby play availability may depend on the release version and operating system.

How do I change advertising consent?

AdMob advertising and UMP consent management are planned for launch. Once available, we plan to let you revisit your choices through privacy or advertising-consent options in the app’s settings, where provided. Menu names may differ in the release version.

You can also review your operating system’s advertising-identifier and tracking settings. Uninstalling and reinstalling the app does not guarantee deletion of all consent records or data held by third-party services.

The game is frozen or the screen looks wrong.
  1. End the current round and start the game again.
  2. If the issue continues, fully close YouIN from the app switcher and reopen it. You may lose the current round.
  3. Restart your device. After launch, also check the store for an app update.

If you still need help, include the device model, operating system version, app version, and steps that led to the problem. Leave out face photos and personal contact information.

Are Caught You and Who More? results reliable?

Treat the results as part of the fun. Caught You is not a scientific lie detector and must not be used for important decisions involving employment, legal matters, medicine, finance, or relationship surveillance.

Who More? reflects fictional NPC preferences that change from game to game. It is not an objective looks rating and does not infer sensitive attributes such as identity, gender, age, race, or skin color.

Are camera videos or face photos saved?

Videos and photos are processed on your device for reaction effects and gameplay. Originals are not uploaded to servers or stored permanently. Temporary face-analysis data is designed to be discarded after a round or when you exit the game. Existing originals selected from your photo library remain in your library.

Advertising SDKs and nearby connections involve separate information handling. See the privacy policy for details.

Still need help?

Tell us your device model, OS and app versions, and what happened. Remove personal information from any screenshots before sharing.

Open a support issue

Our temporary support channel is GitHub Issues. Posts are public and a GitHub account is needed to submit. Do not include face photos, personal contact details, or other sensitive information.
