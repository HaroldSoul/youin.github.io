YouIN is preparing for release. AdMob advertising and UMP consent management are planned for launch. This policy distinguishes those plans from confirmed camera-processing principles.

01. Scope and release status

This policy explains information handling in the YouIN app and official website. The app is preparing for release. Planned features, including advertising and consent management, are identified separately below. If the release version changes how information is handled, we will update this policy before those changes are made public.

This website has no advertising SDK, visitor analytics tool, or camera feature. It may save your language preference in your browser’s local storage. GitHub Pages, which hosts the website, may process web-request information such as IP addresses under its own policy.

02. Information processed

  • Camera and photo information during play: camera footage, photos you capture or select, and temporary face-analysis results are processed on your device for reaction effects and gameplay.
  • Nearby play information: connection information and gameplay messages may be exchanged between participating devices to discover peers and run a game.
  • Advertising information: after an ad SDK is introduced at launch, it may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interactions. See the advertising section below.
  • Support requests: we use information you voluntarily submit, and information made available by the support channel such as your public profile, to help resolve your request. Do not submit face photos or sensitive personal information.

03. Camera and photo-library permissions

Camera permission is used for capture and real-time processing needed for face-reaction effects and gameplay. Photo-library access is used when you choose a photo for a game. Where your operating system supports it, you can grant access only to selected photos.

You can deny or revoke permissions in device settings. Features that need a denied permission may be unavailable or limited. Only use another person’s photo with their consent.

04. On-device face processing

Camera footage and photos you capture or select are processed on your device for reaction effects and gameplay. Original footage and photos are not uploaded to YouIN servers or stored permanently. Temporary face-analysis data is designed to be discarded when a round ends or you exit the game.

We do not infer sensitive attributes such as identity, gender, age, race, or skin color. “Caught You” is an entertainment game, not a scientific lie detector. It must not be used for important decisions involving employment, legal matters, medicine, finance, or relationship surveillance. “Who More?” results reflect fictional NPC preferences that change from game to game, not an objective assessment of appearance.

This camera-processing principle does not mean that all information stays on your device. Nearby gameplay messages, planned advertising SDKs, and website hosting involve separate information handling.

05. Nearby device connections

Last Kick’s nearby play may use Nearby Connections. When you join nearby play, connection information and gameplay messages are exchanged between participating devices. Depending on the operating system and feature, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, nearby-device, or local-network permissions may be needed.

The Google Play services Nearby SDK may process usage diagnostics, including connection-performance metrics and device information. Google says users can manage usage and diagnostics sharing in Google settings on supported Android devices. Connection messages are separate from original camera media; we do not transmit original camera media for nearby connections.

06. Advertising and consent — planned

We plan to introduce Google AdMob advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent management when the service launches. This website does not include those advertising SDKs. This section describes information handling planned for the app at launch.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process advertising and other device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from information such as IP addresses, diagnostics, and interactions such as ad impressions and clicks for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. The specific information depends on the operating system, SDK version, ad settings, and consent status. Processing camera data on-device does not mean that advertising data also stays on-device.

We plan to use UMP to present privacy choices and consent where required, and provide in-app privacy options when changes to those choices are required. Refusing consent does not necessarily stop every kind of processing. Limited advertising or fraud-prevention processing may still apply; refer to the actual consent screen and Google’s policies.

07. Children and guardians

Check the age rating and usage guidance in the app stores at launch. Minors should review camera, photo, nearby-play, and advertising choices with a guardian. Guardians should help manage children’s permissions and app use, and prevent the use or disclosure of other people’s photos without consent.

If your local rules require a guardian’s consent, decide whether to use the app together with a guardian. If you believe a child’s personal information has been unintentionally provided, for example in a support request, contact support. Do not add the child’s name, photos, or contact details to a public issue.

08. Retention and deletion

Original camera footage and photos are not uploaded to servers or stored permanently. Temporary face-analysis data used during a game is designed to be discarded at the end of a round or when the game is exited. Selecting an existing photo does not delete the original from your photo library.

You can delete the website’s language preference by clearing browser storage. Support information is used for as long as necessary to resolve and follow up on a request. Retention and deletion of public posts also depend on the support provider’s features and policies. You can edit or delete your post where supported, or request deletion without repeating sensitive information.

Retention by planned Google advertising services and Nearby services is subject to each provider’s policies and settings. We do not guarantee that YouIN can directly delete all information held by those providers.

09. Third-party services

The following official resources explain each provider’s role:

  • Google Privacy Policy — information handling for AdMob, UMP, and other Google services.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and UMP guidance — planned advertising and consent management.
  • Nearby Connections overview — nearby connections and usage diagnostics.
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement — website hosting and the current public support channel.

Following these links takes you to services governed by their providers’ policies. Google may process advertising and diagnostic information on its own servers. Its policies explain processing locations and details.

10. Your choices

  • Change camera, photo, nearby-device, and local-network permissions in device settings.
  • End a round or exit the app to stop face processing for that game.
  • After launch, revisit consent through in-app privacy options where provided. You can also manage advertising-identifier and tracking settings in your operating system.
  • Manage usage and diagnostics sharing in Google settings on supported devices.
  • Clear browser storage or use KO/EN to change the website’s language preference.
  • Use support to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted. We will review the request and explain what action is available within the relevant service.

11. Policy changes

We will update this page and its revision date when features, SDKs, or actual information handling change. We plan to communicate significant changes through the website, in-app notices, or another appropriate method. Before release, we will review consistency between the app’s actual behavior, app-store privacy disclosures, and this policy.

12. Contact

Use the channel below for YouIN privacy and support requests. A dedicated support email has not yet been published.

Open a support issue

Our temporary support channel is GitHub Issues. Posts are public and a GitHub account is needed to submit. Do not include face photos, personal contact details, or other sensitive information.
